Upon logging into the website, teachers will receive a notification prompting them to grade the submitted assignments.

<video src={require('./video/gradeassignments.mp4').default} controls></video>

1. Click 'Grade'
2. View the assignments and enter comment. 
3. Drag the scroll bar to mark the assignment. Click 'Grade'.
4. You can edit your comment and grade. Click 'Update' when you're finished.
